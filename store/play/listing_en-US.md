Title (29/30):

StimPad - Stimming Soundboard

Short description (76/80):

Full description:

StimPad is a soundboard of everyday noises you can tap and play.

Hear rain, fans, crowds, animals, household sounds, and more. Fourteen sounds are free. StimPad Plus opens the rest of the 95-sound library.

What you can do
- Tap a sound to play it. A loop keeps going until you stop it or a sleep timer ends.
- Preview a locked sound for 8 seconds.
- Watch one ad to open the full library for 20 minutes, once a day.
- Get StimPad Plus, a one-time purchase, to unlock every sound and remove ads.
- Bring a recent sound back with Play again.
- Set a sleep timer for 15, 30, or 60 minutes. Short sounds keep playing until that time.
- Save favorites on this device. No account is required.

StimPad Plus is a single purchase on this Google account. Restore it from Settings after a reinstall, or on another phone signed into the same account.

The free tier shows ads from Google AdMob. Where a choice is required, StimPad asks before the first ad. You can change that choice in Settings.

Playback stops when you leave the app. StimPad does not play on the lock screen or in the background.
