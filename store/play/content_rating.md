# Content rating (IARC)

Select these answers in Play Console. The audience is 13 and older. This is not a Families app.

- Violence: none
- Fear, horror, or sexual content: none
- Language: none
- Controlled substances: none
- User-generated content and public chat: none. Feedback is a private form to the developer.

- Location sharing with other users: none
- Digital purchases: yes. StimPad Plus is one in-app product, bought once.
- Ads: yes, on the free tier. StimPad Plus removes them.
- The app does not let people communicate with one another.
