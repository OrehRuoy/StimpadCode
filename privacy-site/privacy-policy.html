StimPad Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

StimPad ("Audio Sensory Fidget") is published by Brock Hall (OrehRuoy). Contact: hallanhype@gmail.com

Overview

StimPad is a sensory soundboard app. We do not require an account. Favorites and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

  • Local app data: favorites, visual effect preference, purchase unlock state (stored on device).
  • Analytics: Firebase / Google Analytics event data (e.g. screen views, sound plays) to improve the app.
  • Advertising (free tier only): Google AdMob may collect device/ad identifiers to show ads. StimPad Plus removes ads.
  • Purchases: Apple App Store and Google Play process payments; we receive purchase confirmation, not payment card details.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — Firebase Privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — purchase processing

Tracking (iOS)

On iOS, the free tier may request App Tracking Transparency permission for personalized ads. You can deny tracking and still use the app. StimPad Plus removes ads.

Children

StimPad is a general audience app and is not directed solely at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention

Local data remains until you delete the app. Analytics and ad partners retain data per their policies.

Your choices

  • Purchase StimPad Plus to remove ads.
  • Reset tracking preferences in iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
  • Email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
